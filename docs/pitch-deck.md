## Core Idea 1: Optimizing Learner Personalization
- **Course context:** efficient collection and indexing of academic materials,
  provided by the faculty, to ground LLM responses.
- **Personalized data:** secure storage of learner history and performance for
  adaptive, tailored paths.

Core strategy: streamlining data ingestion to fuel real-time educational
insights.

## Core Idea 2: Intelligent Response Generation
- **Behavioral analysis:** processing stored historical data to identify
  learning patterns and performance gaps.
- **Adaptive doubt solving:** generating customized responses for every query
  based on the learner's unique academic journey.

Core strategy: leveraging data-driven intelligence to provide instant, tailored
educational support.

## Core Idea 3: Unified Data Management
- **Context and teacher input:** centralizing course materials and specific
  faculty guidance to ensure educational accuracy.
- **Learner profiling:** capturing personalized learner behavior and progress
  data for high-fidelity tailoring.

Core strategy: combining institutional knowledge with student data for a
unified learning ecosystem.

## Demonstration
**Example situation:** a student goes through a new chapter module and
attempts problems. While working through the material they google some
challenging concepts, then hit a problem that exposes their shaky
understanding. They're stuck and turn to help.

**Traditional LLMs, drawbacks:**
- Generic solution to the question, which can also be found on Google.
- Not aware of the full scope of the text being followed, or the concepts and
  methods it uses.
- The student ends up more puzzled and has to prompt for another half hour to
  get a grasp of the subject.

**LLMs armed with the right context, advantages:**
- Knows the exact confusions the learner faces and works to reinforce
  understanding.
- Uses exact references, like examples and definitions from the reference
  text, with source tags for easy lookup.
- Can follow up with similar questions from the text.
- Stores every issue faced, for future use.
