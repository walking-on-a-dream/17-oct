# Idea brainstorming

Help the team come up with project ideas and keep them recorded in one consistent place: the `Ideas:` section of `README.md`.

## Workflow

1. **Understand the goal.** Before generating ideas, find out (from the request, or by asking briefly) the theme or problem space, the time available (e.g. a weekend hackathon vs. a semester project), and any required tech, APIs or judging criteria. Don't stall on this: if the request is open-ended, state your assumptions and go.
2. **Generate.** Offer 3–5 distinct ideas, not variations of one. For each, give a one-line pitch and say who it is for.
3. **Refine.** When the team picks an idea, flesh it out using the template below. Be honest about risks and scope; cut features until the MVP fits the time available.
4. **Record.** Add the refined idea to the `Ideas:` section of `README.md` using the template. Keep existing ideas intact and append new ones in order. Don't remove or rewrite someone else's idea unless asked.

## Idea template

```markdown
### <Idea name>
- **Pitch:** One sentence on what it does and for whom.
- **Problem:** The pain point it solves.
- **MVP:** The 3–5 features that make a working demo.
- **Stretch goals:** Nice-to-haves if time allows.
- **Tech stack:** Proposed languages, frameworks, APIs.
- **Risks:** What could block or sink it.
- **Proposed by:** <team member(s)>
```

## Team

The team is listed at the top of `README.md`. When an idea comes from a specific person, credit them in **Proposed by**. When splitting MVP work, suggest owners from that list.
